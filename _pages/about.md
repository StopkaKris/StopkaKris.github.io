<section class="home-section">

    <div class="section-heading">
      <div>
        <p class="section-kicker">Research program</p>
        <h2>Mechanistic models, revealing experiments, faster decisions.</h2>
      </div>
      <p>My work connects observations and simulations across length scales—from individual grains and defects to engineering fatigue life. The goal is not only to explain why materials fail, but to build predictive tools that make new materials easier to trust and deploy.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="focus-grid">
      <article class="focus-card">

        <a href="{{ '/research/#fatigue' | relative_url }}">Explore this theme →</a>

        <a href="{{ '/research/#manufacturing' | relative_url }}">Explore this theme →</a>
      </article>
